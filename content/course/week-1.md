# Week 1 — Empathize: Talking to people, talking to AI

*Minggu 1 — Berempati: Berbicara dengan manusia, berbicara dengan AI*

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this week, students can:

- Ask open questions in English to understand someone's daily life and problems
- Describe a person's routine and frustrations using the present simple
- Explain what a prompt is and why context and role change the AI's answer
- Translate a simple Indonesian prompt into English and compare the AI's answers

## Language focus

### 1. Open vs. closed questions

Closed questions get "yes/no". Open questions get stories — and stories give insights.

| ❌ Closed | ✅ Open |
|---|---|
| Do you like Instagram? | **How** do you use Instagram for your business? |
| Is it difficult? | **What** is the most difficult part? |
| Do you have problems? | **Tell me about** the last time this went wrong. |

**Useful follow-ups:** *Why is that? · Can you give me an example? · How did that make you feel? · What did you do next?*

### 2. Present simple for habits and problems

- *She **posts** three times a week.* (habit — remember the **-s**!)
- *They **don't have** time to write captions.*
- *Customers **ask** the same questions every day.*

**Common Indonesian-speaker errors:** *"She post every day"* → *She post**s***; *"They are not have time"* → *They **don't** have time.*

## Session plans

### Session 1 (2 h): What is a prompt? Why English?

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 15 min | **Warm-up:** Students share one thing they used AI for this month (Indonesian is OK). Teacher writes English versions on the board. |
| 25 min | **Demo — same idea, two languages.** Teacher asks an AI tool *"buatkan rencana belajar"* then *"Create a 4-week study plan for a second-year accounting student who has 1 hour per day. Use a table."* Students compare. Discuss: what made the second answer better? (Not only English — the **details**.) |
| 20 min | **Introduce CRAFT** (poster). Focus today on **Context** and **Role**. |
| 30 min | **Prompt Lab, first try.** Each student writes one real prompt in Indonesian, runs it through the Lab, reads the direct translation and the Starter option. Pairs: *"What did the Lab add? Do you agree?"* |
| 20 min | **Wrap-up:** Each student writes in English: *"A good prompt has… because…"* |
| 10 min | Homework briefing |

### Session 2 (2 h): Choosing a challenge & learning to interview

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 15 min | **Challenge areas.** Teacher presents 6 challenge areas (see below). Students form teams of 2–3 and pick one. |
| 25 min | **Language focus:** open vs. closed questions. Drill: turn 10 closed questions into open questions. |
| 30 min | **Write an interview guide** (8–10 open questions in English, with Indonesian notes allowed). Teams use the **Project Studio coach**: *"Here are our interview questions…"* — the coach will ask clarification questions and suggest English upgrades. |
| 30 min | **Practice interviews** in pairs across teams (in English, switching to Indonesian when stuck — then noting the English phrase they needed). |
| 20 min | Reflection: *Which question gave the most interesting answer? Why?* |

**Challenge areas** (students may propose their own):

1. **UMKM / small business** — marketing, customer service, bookkeeping
2. **Education** — helping younger students, teachers, or classmates learn
3. **Tourism & local culture** — guides for a village, *bahasa daerah* preservation
4. **Environment** — waste, flooding, saving energy in a campus or kampung
5. **Health & wellbeing** — student stress, healthy food on a budget
6. **Jobs & careers** — CVs, interview practice, finding internships

### Session 3 (2 h): Empathy maps and insights

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 20 min | **Teams report** on real interviews (homework): 2 minutes each, in English. |
| 30 min | **Empathy map:** Says / Thinks / Does / Feels. Teams fill a poster, then type the key points into the **Empathize canvas** in Project Studio. |
| 30 min | **Socratic round.** Teams chat with the coach. The coach will ask *evidence* questions (*"How do you know? What did they actually say?"*). Teams must answer with interview data, not guesses. |
| 25 min | **Write 3 insights** in English with the pattern: *"We were surprised that… because…"* or *"[User] … but …"* |
| 15 min | Exit ticket: one English sentence that describes your user's biggest pain point. |

## Homework

1. Interview **at least 3 real people** from your target group (15 minutes each). Record notes in English or Indonesian; write the final insights in English.
2. Use the Prompt Lab on **2 prompts** from your daily life. Save the best option to your Portfolio and write a 2-sentence reflection.

## Deliverable checklist

- [ ] Empathize canvas filled in (all 4 fields)
- [ ] Empathy map photo/poster
- [ ] 3 insights in English
- [ ] 2 prompts in the Portfolio with reflections

## Teacher notes

- **Do not let teams pick a solution this week.** Many students arrive with "We will make an app". Ask: *"For whom? How do you know they need it?"* The coach is designed to redirect students back to the user.
- **Indonesian is a resource, not a mistake.** Let students think in Indonesian, then use the Lab to see the English. Ask them to notice *one* phrase they will reuse.
- **Interviews are real-world English practice** — even if the interview happens in Indonesian, the notes, insights and report must be in English.
