## Learning outcomes

By the end of this week, students can:

- Run a short user test in English and record feedback objectively
- Give and receive feedback politely using hedging language
- Check AI output for factual errors, made-up information and bias
- Improve a prompt based on user feedback and explain the change
- Deliver a clear 3-minute pitch in English with a live demo

## Language focus

### 1. Asking for feedback (to testers)

- *Could you try to … and tell me what you think?*
- *What was the most useful / confusing part?*
- *If you could change one thing, what would it be?*
- *On a scale of 1 to 5, how likely are you to use this again? Why?*

### 2. Giving feedback politely (hedging)

| Too direct | Better |
|---|---|
| *This is bad.* | *It **seems** a bit hard to use.* |
| *Change the colour.* | ***It might be better to** use a darker colour.* |
| *Wrong.* | ***I'm not sure** this price is correct — **could you check**?* |

Pattern: **I like… / I wonder… / What if…?**

### 3. Reporting results

- *Three out of five testers **said that** the answers were too long.*
- *Most users **found** the menu search useful.*
- *Based on this feedback, **we decided to** …*

### 4. Signposting a pitch

| Part | Phrases |
|---|---|
| Hook | *Imagine you are… · Did you know that…?* |
| Problem | *We talked to … people. We found that…* |
| Solution | *So we built… · Let me show you how it works.* |
| Demo | *First… · Now I'll type… · As you can see…* |
| Evidence | *When we tested it, …* |
| Prompting | *Our key prompt was… We improved it by…* |
| Next steps | *Next, we want to… · Thank you. Any questions?* |

## Checking AI output — the VERIFY checklist

Before you trust or share AI output, check:

- **V**alid facts — can you confirm names, numbers, dates, prices from a trusted source?
- **E**veryone included — does it fit all your users (gender, region, language, ability)?
- **R**ight tone — would your user feel respected?
- **I**nvented details — any phone numbers, links, quotes or studies that might be made up?
- **F**its the task — does it follow every CRAFT instruction you gave?
- **Y**our judgment — would *you* put your name on it?

## Session plans

### Session 1 (2 h): User testing

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 20 min | **Language focus:** asking for feedback; reporting results. Write the test plan in the **Test canvas** (who, what tasks, which questions). The coach will check your questions are open, not leading. |
| 15 min | Mini-lesson: the **VERIFY** checklist with 3 real AI mistakes. |
| 60 min | **User tests** (in class with invited users, or with other teams acting as the target user). Observe silently; take notes in English. |
| 25 min | Teams sort feedback: *keep / change / add*. Record it in the Test canvas. |

### Session 2 (2 h): Iterate & prepare the pitch

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 50 min | **Improve.** Choose the **one** most important change. Take the prompt to the Prompt Lab, add the user feedback as answers to the clarifying questions, and Refine. Rebuild. Log the before/after in the Portfolio. |
| 20 min | **Language focus:** pitch signposting. Model pitch by the teacher. |
| 40 min | Teams write and rehearse their 3-minute pitch. Peer coaching with *I like / I wonder / What if*. |
| 10 min | Final check: Portfolio has 8+ prompts with reflections. |

### Session 3 (2 h): Demo day

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 75 min | **Pitches** (3 min + 2 min Q&A per team). Audience must ask at least one Socratic question per team. |
| 20 min | **Individual reflection** (in English, 200–300 words) — see prompts below. |
| 15 min | Peer awards: *Best user insight · Best prompt · Best demo · Most improved English*. Celebrate! |
| 10 min | Download Portfolio (.md) and submit. |

**Reflection prompts:**

1. What did you learn about your users that surprised you?
2. Show one prompt that improved a lot. What changed and why?
3. When did the AI make a mistake? How did you notice?
4. What is one English phrase you will keep using? Why?
5. What would you do in the next iteration?

## Deliverable checklist (final submission)

- [ ] All 5 canvases complete; brief regenerated after testing
- [ ] Final prototype (link / screenshots / file)
- [ ] Portfolio export (.md) with **8+ prompts** and reflections, including a before/after pair
- [ ] Pitch slides (max 6) and recording or live delivery
- [ ] Individual reflection (200–300 words, English)

## Teacher notes

- Invite real users (shop owners, younger students, lecturers) to Session 1 — it raises motivation and produces genuine English interaction.
- For the pitch, grade **clarity and effort over accent**. Encourage short sentences and notes on cards.
- Use the [assessment rubric](/course/assessment).
