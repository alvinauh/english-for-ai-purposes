# Assessment & rubrics

*Penilaian & rubrik*

## Weighting

| Component | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Final project** (team) | 30% | Brief, prototype, canvases |
| **Prompt portfolio** (individual) | 25% | 8+ prompts, version history, reflections |
| **Pitch & demo** (team, individually scored on speaking) | 25% | 3-minute pitch + Q&A |
| **Reflection & participation** (individual) | 20% | Final reflection, weekly exit tickets, Socratic questions asked |

## Rubric (each criterion scored 1–4)

### Final project

| Criterion | 4 — Excellent | 3 — Good | 2 — Developing | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **User understanding** | Insights come from 3+ real interviews and clearly shape the project | Based on real interviews; link to project mostly clear | Few interviews or insights are general | No real user evidence |
| **Problem definition** | Clear, specific problem statement and HMW; not a solution in disguise | Clear but slightly broad | Vague or describes a solution | Missing |
| **Prototype** | Works end to end, handles edge cases, improved after testing | Works for the main use case | Partly works | Does not work / not built |
| **Use of AI** | AI has a clear, sensible role; outputs verified | Clear role; some checking | AI role unclear; little checking | Output used without checking |

### Prompt portfolio

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CRAFT quality** | Final prompts use all 5 elements appropriately | 4 elements | 2–3 elements | Mostly one-line prompts |
| **Iteration** | Clear before/after versions with reasons | Some versions with reasons | Versions without reasons | No iteration |
| **Techniques** | Uses 3+ techniques (few-shot, steps, constraints, chain, system prompt) correctly | 2 techniques | 1 technique | None |
| **Reflection English** | Clear explanations using *because / so / if*; few errors | Mostly clear | Short, many errors, but understandable | Missing or not in English |

### Pitch & demo (speaking)

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | Uses signposting; hook → problem → solution → demo → evidence → next steps | Most parts present | Some parts missing | No clear structure |
| **Clarity** | Easy to follow; short, clear sentences | Mostly clear | Often hard to follow | Not understandable |
| **Vocabulary** | Uses course vocabulary accurately (user, insight, prototype, iterate…) | Some course vocabulary | Limited | Very limited |
| **Q&A** | Answers questions with reasons and examples | Answers simply | Needs help to answer | Cannot answer |

### Reflection & participation

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Socratic thinking** | Regularly asks others useful questions of different types | Asks questions sometimes | Rarely asks | Never |
| **Self-awareness** | Specific about what was learned and what to improve | Some specific points | General statements | Missing |

## Notes on fairness

- **Accent is not assessed.** Clarity and communication are.
- **AI use is expected** — what is assessed is the student's *thinking*: decisions, reasons, checking and reflection. A polished prompt with no explanation scores lower than a simpler prompt with a clear explanation of why it was changed.
- For team work, use a short peer-evaluation form to adjust individual scores if needed.
