# Course overview: English for AI Building

*Bahasa Inggris untuk Pembangun AI — kursus 4 minggu*

**English for AI Building** is a 4-week English for Specific Purposes (ESP) course. Students learn English **through** the work of planning and building a real project with AI. They don't study English first and use it later. They use it from day one, to interview people, define problems, write prompts and pitch their work.

> **The big idea:** A good prompt is clear English plus clear thinking. We teach both at the same time.

## Who it is for

- University, polytechnic and SMK students in Indonesia (Cambodia version coming next)
- English level **A2–B1** (can read simple texts, but writing feels slow and unsure)
- No coding needed. Students who can code can build more ambitious prototypes.

## What students produce

By the end of Week 4, every student (or team of 2–3) has:

1. **A project brief** — a one-page plan made with design thinking
2. **A working prototype** built with AI (a chatbot, a set of materials, a website, a campaign, a simple app…)
3. **A prompt portfolio** — at least 8 prompts with the original idea, the improved English prompt and a reflection
4. **A 3-minute pitch** in English, with a live demo

## Three strands, woven together

| Strand | What students learn | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| **Design thinking** | Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test | Project Studio |
| **Prompt engineering** | The CRAFT framework, iteration, examples, step-by-step, prompt chains, checking AI output | Prompt Lab |
| **English for AI** | Question forms, problem statements, modals, imperatives, sequencing, conditionals, feedback and pitch language | Both tools + class |

And running through everything: **Socratic questioning** — asking the questions that make an idea (or a prompt) better.

## The 4 weeks at a glance

| Week | Design thinking stage | English focus | Prompting focus | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [1](/course/week-1) | Empathize | Wh-questions, present simple, describing problems | What a prompt is; Context + Role | Empathy map + 3 insights |
| [2](/course/week-2) | Define + Ideate | Problem statements, "How might we…", modals (could/should/might), comparing | Action verbs, Format, Tone; AI as brainstorm partner | Project brief |
| [3](/course/week-3) | Prototype | Imperatives, sequencing, "so that", conditionals (If… then…) | Few-shot examples, step-by-step, constraints, prompt chains, system prompts | Prototype v1 + prompt log |
| [4](/course/week-4) | Test + Present | Asking for and giving feedback, hedging, signposting a presentation | Evaluating output, hallucination, bias, iteration | Final project, pitch, reflection |

## The two frameworks students use every day

### CRAFT — for prompts

| Letter | Element | Question to ask | Example phrase |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C** | Context | What does the AI need to know? | *I run a small coffee shop in Bandung…* |
| **R** | Role | Who should the AI be? | *Act as an experienced barista trainer.* |
| **A** | Action | What exactly should it do? | *Write a 5-step checklist for…* |
| **F** | Format | What shape should the answer have? | *Use a table with 3 columns.* |
| **T** | Tone | Who is it for, and what style? | *Friendly, for new staff aged 18–20.* |

### Six Socratic question types — for thinking

| Type | Ask… | Indonesian |
|---|---|---|
| Clarification | *What do you mean by…?* | Apa maksudmu dengan…? |
| Assumptions | *What are you assuming about…?* | Apa yang kamu asumsikan tentang…? |
| Evidence | *How do you know? What did users say?* | Bagaimana kamu tahu? |
| Viewpoints | *How would a [teacher/seller/parent] see this?* | Bagaimana menurut sudut pandang…? |
| Implications | *If this works, what happens next? What could go wrong?* | Jika berhasil, lalu apa? Apa risikonya? |
| The question | *Is this the right question to ask?* | Apakah ini pertanyaan yang tepat? |

## How the app fits in

- **Project Studio** — students fill in a design-thinking canvas. A Socratic coach asks them questions (in English, with Indonesian support), suggests English improvements, and never gives them the answer. At the end it turns the canvas into a **project brief**.
- **Prompt Lab** — students write a rough prompt in Indonesian, English or both. The Lab shows: what the AI understood, a direct translation, a CRAFT check, **three expanded English prompts** (Starter, Structured, Advanced) each with an Indonesian translation, Socratic questions to refine it, vocabulary and a grammar tip.
- **Portfolio** — saved prompts with reflections, exportable for assessment.

> **Teaching principle:** The app expands and translates, but the student decides. Every week, students must explain *why* they chose or changed a prompt — in English.

## Time

- **3 sessions per week × 2 hours** in class (24 hours total)
- **2–3 hours per week** independent work (interviews, building, portfolio)
- Can be compressed into an intensive 2-week bootcamp (6 sessions/week) or stretched to 8 weeks (1.5 sessions/week).
